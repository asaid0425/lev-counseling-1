01 the short version

not the kind who just / sits and nods.

I provide integrative, relationship-based psychotherapy for individuals and couples navigating the unique pressures of high-stakes environments — work grounded in clarity, discretion, and results, not rigid protocols.

Rather than relying on manuals, I focus on identifying the patterns in thinking, emotion, behavior, and relationships that keep people stuck. Once those patterns are clear, we work directly to change how they function in real time — with an emphasis on precision, relational intelligence, and long-term resilience.

My style is direct, engaged, and active. I'm a strategic partner in your progress, offering an honest perspective and real tools for change — while creating a nonjudgmental space where meaningful exploration can actually happen. Having worked with thousands of clients from diverse backgrounds, I take cultural context seriously, and I build every treatment plan around exactly what's right for you.
